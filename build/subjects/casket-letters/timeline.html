People and bodies involved

  • Mary, Queen of Scots: Queen of Scotland; the letters are said to be hers, to the Earl of Bothwell.
  • James Hepburn, Earl of Bothwell: Accused of Darnley's murder, acquitted 12 April 1567; married Mary on 15 May 1567 (Henderson).
  • Henry Stewart, Lord Darnley: Mary's husband; killed in the explosion at Kirk-o'-Field.
  • James Douglas, Earl of Morton: Received the casket on 20 June 1567 (his own declaration, Westminster, Dec 1568); executed 1581.
  • James Stewart, Earl of Moray (Regent): Took the casket from the Privy Council on 16 Sept 1568 and carried it to England.
  • William Cecil: Elizabeth's secretary; the French copies at Hatfield come from his papers.
  • T. F. Henderson: Scholar whose 1889 book is the main source read here; argued for the letters' genuineness.
