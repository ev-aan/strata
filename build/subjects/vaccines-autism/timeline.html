People and bodies involved

  • Andrew Wakefield: Lead author of the 1998 paper; struck off the UK register in May 2010 (BMJ c2803); abandoned his appeal ([2012] EWHC 503 para 1).
  • John Walker-Smith: Senior co-author; struck off in 2010; the High Court quashed that decision on 7 Mar 2012.
  • Brian Deer: Journalist whose comparison of the paper with hospital records was published by the BMJ in Jan 2011.
  • William W. Thompson: CDC scientist and co-author of DeStefano 2004; issued a statement through his lawyers on 27 Aug 2014.
  • A child (the Poling case): Not named here: a minor at the time and the injured party (owner rule, log L-19). Child whose compensation case the US government conceded in Nov 2007 (as reported).
  • Robert F. Kennedy Jr.: Author of 'Deadly Immunity' (2005); US Secretary of Health and Human Services from 2025; said he ordered the Nov 2025 CDC page change (STAT).
  • Mark Geier: Co-author of papers reporting an association; Maryland licence revoked 22 Aug 2012 (court record).
