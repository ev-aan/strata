People and bodies involved

  • Captain: Reported as Capt. Smit Machchhar, from India (CNN). Stabbed; surgery; reported in stable condition (a local politician in his home district, via CNN).
  • First officer (suspect): Not publicly identified by authorities. Israel's Prime Minister says he is an Omani national; reported stabbing the captain and trying to crash the plane (Israeli claims); Saudi ministry: 'an assault on the captain by the co-pilot, resulting in various injuries to both'. Taken to Abu Dhabi. Not charged in anything read.
  • Two off-duty pilots: Passengers who took the controls. Netanyahu: 'another air crew'; Flydubai: 'on-duty Flydubai crew travelling on the flight'; CNN: 'reserve pilots', details not shared. Identities unconfirmed.
  • Yaniv Hayoun (passenger): Says he held the attacker in a chokehold and dragged him from the cockpit, then briefly held the controls (CNN, his own account to Netanyahu).
  • Dr. Shota Musaev (passenger): A dentist; says he tied the attacker with headphone cords and zip ties and treated the captain (CNN).
  • Other passengers quoted: Mika Bauman, Assaf Rajuan (CNN); Dalia Shpigel (Haaretz via Al Jazeera). About 174 on board, 27 children, mostly Israeli; four US-Israeli dual citizens; two Australians.
  • Cabin crew: Unnamed; a crew member is seen calling for a doctor and for a pilot in verified video (CNN).
  • Benjamin Netanyahu: Israel's Prime Minister; made the main public claims about the attack, the suspect's nationality and 'Islamist radical indoctrination'; also said it was too early to declare a motive (Al Jazeera).
  • Isaac Herzog: Israel's President; called it a 'great miracle'; said Israel and the UAE will investigate in depth together.
  • Miri Regev: Israel's Transportation Minister; demanded Flydubai's Israel flights be suspended and an inquiry into the UAE agreement (ThePrint).
  • Danny Danon: Israel's UN ambassador; said there was 'no doubt' it was an attempt to bring the plane down (Al Jazeera).
  • Donald Trump: US President; said an Iran link was likely 'based on what I'm hearing', gave no detail (CNN live blog).
  • Penny Wong: Australian Foreign Minister; confirmed two Australian passengers (CNN).
  • Flydubai spokesperson: 'An altercation'; motives 'unknown'; asked all parties to 'refrain from premature speculation' (Al Jazeera).
  • Saudi Interior Ministry: Statement 1 Oct: assault by the co-pilot on the captain, both injured; both left for Abu Dhabi with an Emirati team (CNN).
  • Israeli agencies: Shin Bet, Mossad, the military and the transport ministry are involved in the investigation (Israeli official to CNN).
  • UAE authorities: Say they are investigating whether the violence was linked to terrorism; received the suspect (CNN live blog).
  • Flightradar24: Tracking-data provider; the source of most timing in Panel A.
