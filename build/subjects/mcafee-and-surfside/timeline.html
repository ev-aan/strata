People and bodies involved

  • John McAfee: Software entrepreneur; died aged 75 in a prison cell near Barcelona on 23 June 2021.

  • Catalan justice department: Said 'Everything points to death by suicide' (PolitiFact).
  • Spanish court: Approved extradition on 23 June 2021; later closed the inquiry into the death in a ruling dated 24 July 2023 (AFP).
  • NIST investigators: Judith Mitrani-Reiser (lead) and Glenn Bell (co-lead) presented the findings (NIST).
  • Daniella Levine Cava: Miami-Dade County mayor; said there was nothing to indicate foul play at that point (USA Today).
