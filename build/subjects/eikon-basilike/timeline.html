People and bodies involved

  • Charles I: King of England, 1600-1649; executed 30 January 1649 (bounty page). Authorship of the book is attributed to him by royalists.
  • John Gauden: Bishop, 1605-1662 (TCP authority record); claimed after 1660 to have written the book.
  • John Milton: 1608-1674; answered the book in Eikonoklastes for Parliament.
  • John Grismond and Richard Royston: Printer and publisher of the book and of the Newcastle papers.
