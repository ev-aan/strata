102 Aug 1964 08:05 (approx.)2 Aug: Maddox fires three warning rounds; North Vietnamese boats attack
Hanyok: about 1505 Gulf time (08:05 UTC) the Maddox fired three rounds to warn off the boats; the administration did not report this and said the Vietnamese boats fired first.
