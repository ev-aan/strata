## Review notes (this session, 2026-10-02)
1. **The chat's central blocker is not a blocker.** It says Sethe's critical edition could not be fetched. Sethe's *Die altaegyptischen Pyramidentexte* is public domain and on the Internet Archive: identifiers `diealtaegyptisch03sethuoft` (1908, vols 3-4: critical apparatus, description of the inscriptions, concordance, epigraphy) and `die-altaegyptischen-pyramidentexte-nach-den-papierabdrucken-und-photographien-des-berliner-museums` (1922 print), each with a PDF (about 25 MB) and an OCR text layer. The cloud session can download both. The hieroglyphs are printed signs in page images, so the OCR text will not contain them: reading them needs the page images (or a digital corpus such as the Thesaurus Linguae Aegyptiae, whose website is reachable). Not yet done.
2. **'No pictorial images' needs checking.** It answers the owner's first question ('what are the images'). The chat's own source was a tourism site. Burial-chamber decoration may include more than text and a star ceiling (painted or carved wall panelling is known in some pyramid chambers); this has not been checked for Teti and should be read from an excavation or architectural description (Lauer, Leclant, Labrousse) before it is stated.
3. **Source quality.** The chat's evidence for the chamber came from Wikipedia, Grokipedia, Madain Project, Memphis Tours, Egypt Fun Tours, History Skills and Pyramidofman. None is an acceptable anchor (Wikipedia is orientation only under the project rule). The counts and claims cited from them (301 spells vs 283; the dd-mdw monogram first used under Teti; the Osiris shift) are unchecked.
4. **Utterance numbers and locations** (for example 'PT 373 names Teti', 'sarcophagus carries PT 1-7', wall assignments, page range 205-216) came from secondary sources and need Sethe's concordance and Allen (2005) to confirm. The same applies to the chat's reported Allen-Piankoff discrepancy at PT 247.
5. **Schema.** The chat's fields were designed without the live schema; they would need reconciliation with build/SCHEMA.md and the conformance check (slug IDs, evidence classes, authenticity blocks for the sources, timeline rules T1-T7).
