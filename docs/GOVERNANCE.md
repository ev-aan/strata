# Who decides, and where others contribute

**The owner has the final say on everything in Stratah:** what is dug, what is published, what the rules are, and what a finding says. Nothing becomes public without the owner's decision.

**Contribution is designed in, at defined points.** Others can propose, challenge, supply evidence, review and (in future) donate an agent's work. Their contributions are weighed on the evidence, never on numbers or agreement, and the owner decides what is admitted.

| Point | Who can do it | Who decides |
|---|---|---|
| Suggest a topic, dig or News Review | Anyone (issue form) | Owner opens it |
| Challenge a finding or add evidence | Anyone, if it names one claim and gives a source we can check (docs/CHALLENGES.md) | Admission test S1 to S5, then the normal review; the owner has the final say |
| Do a dig step or a whole dig | Trusted collaborators; later, outside contributors and donated agents, by pull request | Validator, then an independent reviewer who is not the author; owner publishes |
| Review a dig | Independent reviewers (never the author) | Owner accepts the publish decision |
| Change the rules, schema, validator or this file | Anyone may propose | **Owner only.** Agents never merge these |
| Publish to the site | Nobody directly | Owner (the `publish` list and a passed `review.yaml`) |

Review is a dialogue, as in academic peer review: the reviewer sends specific requests back to the submitting agent, the author answers each one, and an independent reviewer resolves them before anything is published (docs/PUBLISH_GATE.md, Revision rounds). Our difference is that publishing is not the end: findings stay open to challenge, new evidence and correction, and every change goes through the same loop.

Rules that follow from this:
- Counts, reactions, popularity and repetition carry no weight. A contribution is judged on its sources.
- Agents and automation never admit a challenge, publish, or change a rule; they prepare, check and report.
- Every decision, including a declined challenge, is logged with its reason.
- Contributors are credited by their contribution record, not by a name on the page.
