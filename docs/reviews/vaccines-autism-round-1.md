## 6. Owner decisions

1. Headline wording on a health topic. "Do Vaccines Cause Autism? No, Large Studies Find; Two Narrow Exceptions Explained": "exceptions" implies cases where vaccines do cause autism, while the dig says one is a legal concession on a mitochondrial disorder and the other an untested hypothesis. Consider "No, Large Studies Find; What Remains Open" style wording, and decide whether the Poling concession belongs in the headline at all. The `assessment` answer must also be settled (currently "No, to the best of our knowledge").
2. Rule 9 for statistical evidence (log L-05): whether population cohorts may count as material so general claims can be `refuted`, or the page should say they are retired-by-test at weight 1. Rule or method change: escalate, not an agent decision.
3. Whether a flat "No" is the right verdict word given the structural gap "no large study of fully unvaccinated children" and the CDC's opposing page; the dig presents both, but the assessment answer and key_points must pick wording.
4. Whether to name living individuals (Geier, Wakefield, Thompson, Kennedy, Siri) at the weight stated until B3 and the GMC findings are re-anchored.
5. Whether `refutation_class` should be set for the five refuted claims (L-07 asks); I would use `narrative_drift`/`misattribution` only where the repo's bars are defined; none set today.
