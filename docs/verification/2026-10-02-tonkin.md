## New evidence worth the owner's attention
- **Bundy memo, 8 a.m., 5 Aug:** evidence for the second attack "is less than we had yesterday... eliminating double counting and mistaken signals... This much seemed certain: There was an attack." And: he "welcomed the recent events as justification for a resolution the Administration had wanted for some time."
- **McNamara, 9:43 a.m., 4 Aug,** before any attack was reported: the ship "is allegedly... to be attacked tonight."
- **McNamara and Congress:** privately on 3 Aug that 34-A "had bearing on" the Maddox engagement; on 6 Aug told senators the Navy "was not aware of, any South Vietnamese actions" (the latter via Prados).
- **Original Vietnamese text of the key report is missing.** Hanyok: "unusual"; Tordella could not produce it in 1972.
- **Report 13** (04/1644Z) mentions "two enemy airplanes" and "two comrades", not ships.
